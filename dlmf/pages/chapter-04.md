## Purpose

Chapter 4 documents elementary functions, including logarithmic, exponential, power, trigonometric, hyperbolic, and inverse functions, plus applications and computation.

## Sections Listed

- 4.1 Special Notation
- 4.2-4.13 Logarithm, exponential, powers, generalized logarithms and exponentials, and Lambert W-function
- 4.14-4.27 Trigonometric functions and inverse trigonometric functions
- 4.28-4.41 Hyperbolic functions and inverse hyperbolic functions
- 4.42-4.44 Applications
- 4.45-4.48 Computation

## Documentation Notes

This is a high-connectivity chapter: later special-function pages often depend on elementary branches, analytic continuation, series, identities, and inverse functions defined here.
