# Preface

Source: [https://dlmf.nist.gov/front/preface](https://dlmf.nist.gov/front/preface)

Observed version: 1.2.7, release date 2026-06-15.

## Purpose

The Preface is the original 2010 project preface. It explains the DLMF and printed handbook as a replacement and extension of the 1964 Abramowitz and Stegun handbook.

## Main Points

- The project was conceived at NIST in 1996.
- Its two stated goals were to create an authoritative replacement handbook and to disseminate essentially the same information from a NIST public website.
- Editorial responsibility was held by Frank W. J. Olver, Daniel W. Lozier, Ronald F. Boisvert, and Charles W. Clark.
- Associate editors, chapter authors, validators, and technical staff all had explicit roles.
- Validation was a major quality mechanism: independent reviewers checked accuracy and coverage before publication.
- The web version includes features such as active links, formula encodings, mathematical search, and interactive graphics.
- The preface notes that even with extensive validation, a reference of this size can still contain errors.

## Documentation Notes

This page is valuable for understanding DLMF governance, authorship, validation, and the distinction between the printed handbook and online library.
