# Foreword

Source: [https://dlmf.nist.gov/front/foreword](https://dlmf.nist.gov/front/foreword)

Observed version: 1.2.7, release date 2026-06-15.

## Purpose

The Foreword positions the DLMF as the successor to the 1964 NIST/NBS Handbook of Mathematical Functions edited by Milton Abramowitz and Irene A. Stegun.

## Main Points

- The original handbook became a major reference for scientists and engineers using special functions.
- A key value of the original handbook was not just collecting formulas, but standardizing definitions and notation.
- DLMF extends that reference role into a web-native form with interactive and technical features.
- The project required broad collaboration among mathematicians, information technologists, and physical scientists.
- The Foreword is signed by Patrick D. Gallagher, then Director of NIST, dated November 20, 2009.
