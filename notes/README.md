# Prime Exclusion and Spectral Observation

## Purpose of these notes

The purpose of these notes is to change the viewpoint. Instead of looking only at the integers that remain prime, we examine the periodic divisibility structures that remove the composites. We then ask how this discrete exclusion field appears when it is observed through the spectral formulas of the zeta function.

The starting point is the elementary sieve. Every prime $p$ supplies a periodic mask

$$
B_p(n)=
\begin{cases}
0,&p\mid n,\\
1,&p\nmid n.
\end{cases}
$$

The masks are individually simple. Their recursive superposition produces the complete prime-exclusion field. This field can then be translated into other representations: residue classes, roots of unity, Fourier modes, logarithmic scale, Mellin transforms, Euler products, prime-power weights, and zeta-zero modes.

The project studies what remains invariant across those translations.

## The critical-line observer

One of the main explorations is:

> **A zero observes the prime field without scale distortion if and only if it observes from the critical line.**

For a nontrivial zero

$$
\rho=\beta+i\gamma,
$$

the associated mode has the form

$$
x^\rho=x^\beta e^{i\gamma\log x}.
$$

Functional reflection pairs the amplitude scales $x^\beta$ and $x^{1-\beta}$. Their geometric mean is $\sqrt{x}$, so the reflection-balanced normalized mode is

$$
\mathcal A_\rho(x)
=
\frac{x^\rho}{\sqrt{x}}
=
x^{\beta-1/2}e^{i\gamma\log x}.
$$

Its amplitude is preserved at every scale precisely when

$$
|\mathcal A_\rho(x)|=x^{\beta-1/2}=1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Re}(\rho)=\frac12.
$$

Thus complete mask transparency defines the prime state, while critical-line amplitude preservation defines its aligned spectral observation. Away from the critical line, the observer sees a scale-distorted state rather than the prime state itself.

## From a critical-line identity to a representation proof

A proof strategy should not derive the Riemann Hypothesis merely from another appearance of $1/2$. Showing that

$$
|x^{\rho-1/2}|=1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Re}(\rho)=\frac12
$$

characterizes the critical line, but a characterization alone does not establish that every nontrivial zero must satisfy it.

The stronger direction explored here is:

> **The Riemann Hypothesis should emerge because a single arithmetic object cannot satisfy all of its exact representation contracts unless $\operatorname{Re}(\rho)=1/2$.**

The arithmetic object is the recursively generated prime-exclusion field. Each mathematical representation exposes a different invariant of that same field. A valid spectral realization must satisfy all of these invariants simultaneously.

## Representation contracts

The proposed contracts are:

| Contract | Status | Structure that must be preserved |
|---|---|---|
| **Binary** | Exact | Every local mask has values in $\{0,1\}$: a position is excluded or transparent. |
| **Periodic** | Exact | Each prime mask satisfies $B_p(n+p)=B_p(n)$. |
| **Prime-local** | Exact | Each new exclusion family is generated by one prime, and its first new composite exclusion occurs at $p^2$. |
| **CRT-compatible** | Exact | A finite collection of prime masks combines into residue classes modulo the primorial $P_k=\prod_{j\le k}p_j$. |
| **Recursive** | Exact | The next prime is discovered from the preceding survivor field and then generates the next exclusion wave. |
| **Multiplicative** | Exact | The complete structure agrees with unique factorization, Euler's product, and the prime-power expansion of $-\zeta'/\zeta$. |
| **Fourier-dual** | Exact | Each periodic divisibility mask has an exact roots-of-unity expansion into pure phase modes. |
| **Mellin-dual** | Exact | Multiplicative scale becomes additive frequency in $\log x$, with $p^{-s}=e^{-s\log p}$. |
| **Reflection-compatible** | Exact | The spectral representation respects the completed-zeta symmetry $\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)$. |
| **Non-amplifying** | To be derived | A faithful normalized observer introduces no scale-dependent growth or decay relative to the reflection-balanced scale $\sqrt{x}$. |

For the proposed route to avoid circularity, the non-amplifying condition cannot simply be assumed: it is already equivalent to $\operatorname{Re}(\rho)=1/2$ for an individual mode. It must instead emerge from the exact contracts when they are simultaneously preserved by the spectral closure of the prime-exclusion field.

The proposal is not that one contract independently proves the Riemann Hypothesis. The critical line should arise as the intersection of all admissible spectral constraints:

$$
\boxed{
\text{admissible prime spectrum}
=
\bigcap_{\text{contracts }C}
\text{spectra satisfying }C.}
$$

The research question is whether this intersection forces

$$
\operatorname{Re}(\rho)=\frac12
$$

for every nontrivial zeta-zero mode.

## The central statement to establish

The goal is not another identity involving $1/2$. It is the single structural statement

> **Every nontrivial zeta-zero mode belongs to the spectral closure of the recursively generated prime-exclusion field.**

Conceptually, let $S_k$ denote the survivor field after the first $k$ prime exclusions and let $\mathcal T_k$ translate that finite field into its Fourier–Mellin representation. The intended spectral closure has the form

$$
\overline{
\bigcup_{k\ge0}\mathcal T_k(S_k)
}^{\,\mathrm{spec}}.
$$

The target statement says that every mode $x^\rho$ occurring in the zeta explicit formula is contained in this completed spectral object.

To make the statement a theorem, the following ingredients could be defined and proved:

1. **An exact finite intertwiner:** construct $\mathcal T_k$ from the finite prime-sieve field to its Fourier–Mellin representation.
2. **Recursive compatibility:** prove that $\mathcal T_{k+1}$ respects the self-generating $p^2$ sieve recursion.
3. **A topology or operator domain:** define precisely what spectral closure and convergence mean as $k\to\infty$.
4. **Spectral completeness:** prove that every nontrivial zeta-zero mode—and no unaccounted mode—appears in that closure.
5. **Exact-contract preservation:** prove that every mode in the closure inherits the binary, periodic, CRT, multiplicative, duality, and reflection constraints.
6. **Emergent non-amplification:** derive the non-amplifying condition from those jointly preserved exact contracts rather than assuming it independently.
7. **Critical-line consequence:** apply the observer equivalence to conclude that the resulting spectral constraints permit only $\operatorname{Re}(\rho)=1/2$.

The intended route is therefore

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\text{finite periodic masks}
\longrightarrow
\text{exact Fourier representation}\\
&\longrightarrow
\text{Mellin-scale representation}
\longrightarrow
\text{recursive spectral closure}\\
&\longrightarrow
\text{all zeta-zero modes satisfy every contract}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Re}(\rho)=\frac12.
\end{aligned}}
$$

If the closure statement follows from an exact finite sieve–Fourier–Mellin intertwiner together with the self-generating $p^2$ recursion, the representation contracts become a route toward a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis rather than another characterization of its critical line.
