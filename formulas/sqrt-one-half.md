# Square Root Of One Half

The square root of `1/2` is an exact irrational value that can be written in several
equivalent forms:

$$
\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
=
\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
=
\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}.
$$

As a decimal, it is approximately:

$$
0.7071.
$$

## 1. Observations

The square root of:

$$
\frac{1}{2}
$$

is exactly:

$$
\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}
$$

or equivalently:

$$
\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.
$$

As a decimal, this is approximately:

$$
0.707.
$$

This is a bit of a special case because:

$$
\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}
$$

does not simplify to a neat whole number or simple rational fraction. Instead, it
becomes an exact expression involving:

$$
\sqrt{2}.
$$

---

## 2. Mathematical Notation

**Fraction form:**

$$
\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
=
\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.
$$

**Decimal form:**

$$
\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}\approx0.7071.
$$

---

## 3. Why The Forms Are Equivalent

Start by separating the square root of the numerator and denominator:

$$
\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}}
=
\frac{\sqrt{1}}{\sqrt{2}}
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}.
$$

Then rationalize the denominator by multiplying top and bottom by `sqrt(2)`:

$$
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
\cdot
\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}}
=
\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.
$$

And since dividing by `2` is the same as multiplying by `1/2`:

$$
\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
=
\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}.
$$

So all three forms name the same value.

---

## 4. What To Notice

The decimal approximation is useful for measurement, but the exact expression carries
more structure. Writing:

$$
\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
$$

keeps the connection to `sqrt(2)` visible. That is why this value appears naturally in
right triangles, the unit circle, and trigonometric values at `45` degrees.
