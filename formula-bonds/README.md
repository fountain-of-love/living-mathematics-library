# Formula Bonds

This folder collects focused pages about how mathematical formulas connect to each
other. A formula bond is a guided explanation of why two mathematical ideas keep
appearing together.

Repo-wide formula index: [Formula Registry](../notes/formula-registry.md).

Each page treats one "bond": not only two formulas sitting near each other, but the
specific invariant, transformation, recurrence, or representation that makes the
connection real.

The pages are written as teaching notes. Each one should help the reader notice:

- what the individual elements are before any formulas appear;
- what ordinary situation or visual intuition the elements reflect;
- which formula is doing the connecting work;
- what changes in the reader's understanding after the bond is seen.

Use the [Formula Registry](../notes/formula-registry.md) when you want a repo-wide index of
individual formulas. Use this folder when you want the story between formulas.

## Page Pattern

A formula-bond page should usually contain:

- a guiding question;
- navigation back to this overview and the registry;
- an "elements at a glance" table;
- ordinary examples before formal notation;
- observation checkpoints after important formulas;
- a correction section when a common claim is close but too broad;
- a final mental image that the reader can carry forward.

## Quality Bar

Before considering a bond page finished, review it with these questions:

- Can a motivated beginner tell why this page exists before meeting the first formula?
- Are the mathematical elements introduced as ideas before they appear as symbols?
- Does each important formula answer a question the text has already raised?
- Are observations separated from claims, so the reader can see what is being noticed?
- Are broad statements corrected where the precise mathematical claim is narrower?
- Does the page link back to the registry and to any source pages that give more detail?
